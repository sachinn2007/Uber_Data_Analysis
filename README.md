
# 🚕 Uber Ride Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Uber Ride Data Analysis is a Data Analytics project based on Uber ride
booking data. The main objective of this project is to analyze booking
patterns and identify useful insights related to ride categories,
purposes, booking times, months, days, and trip distances.

In this project, **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn** are
used for data cleaning, processing, analysis, and visualization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main purpose of this project is to answer important business and
analytical questions from the Uber ride dataset.

# 📊 Key Insights

### 🚗 1. Most Booked Category

**Business** is the most frequently booked category, with around **400
rides**, while Personal rides represent a much smaller share.

### 🎯 2. Primary Purpose of Rides

The most common recorded purpose is **"Not"**, followed by **Meeting**
and **Meal/Entertainment**.

### ⏰ 3. Peak Booking Time

Uber bookings are highest during the **Afternoon**, followed closely by
the **Evening**. Morning has comparatively lower demand.

### 📅 4. Low Demand Months

The monthly analysis shows comparatively lower ride demand during
**July**, with other low-demand periods appearing around **March and
December**.

### 🗓️ 5. Busiest Day of the Week

**Friday** is the busiest day, with approximately **85 rides**, while
**Sunday** has the lowest booking count at around **47 rides**.

### 🛣️ 6. Typical Trip Distance

Most Uber trips are relatively short, with the typical trip distance
centered around **5--6 miles**. Longer trips appear as outliers in the
data.

# 💡 Conclusion

This analysis shows that Uber ride demand is strongly influenced by
**ride category, purpose, time of day, month, and day of the week**.
Business rides dominate the dataset, while afternoon and evening are the
most active booking periods. Friday records the highest daily demand,
and most trips are short-distance journeys.

These insights can help with **📈 demand forecasting, 🚗 driver
allocation, 🎯 targeted promotions, and ⚙️ operational planning**.
